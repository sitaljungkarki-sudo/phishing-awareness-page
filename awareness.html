Red flags you should have noticed

1
Suspicious URL — The address bar showed "Not secure" and the domain was not an official verified address.
2
Urgency language — Phrases like "your account requires verification" are classic pressure tactics used to make you act fast without thinking.
3
Generic branding — Legitimate services always use their real verified domain, not look-alike names.
4
No HTTPS padlock — Real login pages always encrypt your connection. No padlock = danger.
5
Unsolicited request — Did you actually ask to log in? Phishing pages are usually reached through fake emails or links.
Pro tip: Always check the full URL, verify HTTPS, and never enter credentials on a page you arrived at through an unexpected email or message link. When in doubt — don't click!
